# Role Manual: Guest & Public Visitor

The **Guest** role represents public visitors, yacht owners browsing services, and customers placing postal hardware orders or requesting custom panel fabrications.

---

## Key Workflows

### 1. Custom Switch Panel CAD Configurator
- **Route:** `/#designer` (`Switch Panels` tab)
- **Objective:** Design bespoke marine acrylic panels with live cutout geometry and instant quotation.
- **Procedures:**
  1. Open the **Switch Panels** tab from the top navigation bar.
  2. Set custom width (150mm–500mm) and height (100mm–400mm) for flush console mounting.
  3. Drag and drop Contura waterproof rocker switches, USB-C chargers, and digital voltmeters.
  4. Review live pricing and click **Add to Basket** or **Request Formal Quote**.
- **Marine Safety Tip:** Ensure at least 25mm clearance between component cutouts and panel edges for structural integrity against wave vibration.

### 2. Weathered Switchboard Photo Replica Quoter
- **Route:** `/#replica` (`Replica Panels` tab)
- **Objective:** Upload photos of aged or broken boat electrical panels for 1:1 laser replica quoting.
- **Procedures:**
  1. Upload a clear head-on photograph with a ruler or coin for scale reference.
  2. Select panel architecture preset (Compact Tender, Offshore Cruiser, Twin Engine Flybridge).
  3. Enter contact details and berth location to submit the job to the workshop CAD queue.

### 3. Consignment Tracking
- **Route:** `/#tracker` (`Track Order` tab)
- **Objective:** Monitor the repair and delivery milestones of mail-in alternator or panel rebuilds.
- **Procedures:**
  1. Enter 13-character Royal Mail Tracked reference (e.g. `GB-123456789-RM`) or AJW workshop consignment code.
  2. Inspect real-time timeline from Workshop Intake to Out for Delivery.
