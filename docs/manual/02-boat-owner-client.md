# Role Manual: Boat Owner (Client)

The **Client** role is designated for verified vessel owners across Solent marinas (Haslar, Gosport, Port Solent, River Hamble, Cowes, Chichester).

---

## Key Workflows

### 1. Vessel Profile & Electrical Specification
- **Route:** `/#portal` (`Vessel Portal` tab)
- **Objective:** Maintain vessel technical data, battery bank capacities, and marina berth details.
- **Procedures:**
  1. Open the **Vessel Portal** tab from the top navigation.
  2. Specify system voltage (12V or 24V) and service battery bank chemistry (LiFePO4 Lithium, AGM, Gel, or Flooded Lead-Acid).
  3. Enter capacity (e.g. 400Ah) and inverter wattage specifications.
  4. Click **Export Electrical Summary (PDF)** to generate an insurer-approved compliance certificate and wiring schematic.
- **Marine Safety Tip:** Always verify alternator charging regulation (e.g. Sterling Power or Victron BMS control) before transitioning from lead-acid to drop-in lithium banks.

### 2. Pontoon Diagnostic Review & Digital Customer Sign-Off
- **Route:** `/#portal` (`Work Orders` subtab)
- **Objective:** Review field technician diagnostic findings, inspect photo proofs, and approve completed jobs.
- **Procedures:**
  1. Open the active job discussion thread to inspect chronological inspection notes and photos.
  2. Review labor billed at flat £25/hour and listed marine tinned copper materials.
  3. Sign electronically on the canvas sign-off pad to finalize the work order and trigger warranty coverage.
